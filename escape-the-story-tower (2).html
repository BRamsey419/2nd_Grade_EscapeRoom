🏰 Escape the Story Tower

A reading & language adventure for Grade 2

📖✨

The Dragon of Story Tower

Long ago, a friendly (but very forgetful) dragon locked the Great Book of Tales at the top of Story Tower with three magic locks. To open each lock, you'll need to solve a room full of word puzzles. Finish all three rooms, then face the Final Quiz Chamber to escape with the book!

🔑 Room 1 of 3

The Vocabulary Vault

Each treasure chest is guarded by a tricky word. Read the clue sentence carefully, then choose what the bold word means. Get all five correct to unlock the vault!

🔑 Room 2 of 3

The Whispering Library

Read the story below. You can read it yourself, or press the button to have it read aloud. Then answer the questions to prove you understood it.

Ruby the Raccoon

Ruby was a curious raccoon who lived near a quiet pond. Every night, she tiptoed out of her cozy den to explore the forest. One evening, Ruby found a shiny silver spoon by the water. She washed it carefully in the pond, just like raccoons like to do.

Suddenly, she heard a soft splash. It was Ruby's best friend, Oliver the Otter! Oliver was looking for his lost spoon. "Is this yours?" asked Ruby, holding up the shiny spoon. Oliver's eyes lit up with joy. "Yes! Thank you, Ruby!" he said. The two friends laughed and played by the pond until the moon rose high in the sky.

🔑 Room 3 of 3

The Grammar Forge

The forge fixes broken sentences. Choose the correct verb or the correctly written sentence to hammer each one into shape.

🏆 Final Challenge

The Treasure Chamber

You've earned all three keys! Answer these final questions to open the Great Book of Tales. You'll need at least 6 out of 8 correct to escape.

Common Core alignment (Grade 2 ELA):
  • RL.2.1 — Ask and answer who/what/where/when/why/how questions to demonstrate understanding of key details in a text (Reading Room comprehension questions).
  • RL.2.5 — Describe the overall structure of a story, including how the beginning introduces the story and the ending concludes the action (Reading Room sequencing question).
  • RF.2.4 — Read with sufficient accuracy and fluency to support comprehension (optional read-aloud support in the Reading Room).
  • L.2.4 — Determine the meaning of unknown words using context clues (Vocabulary Vault).
  • L.2.1 — Demonstrate command of standard English grammar, including forming and using frequently occurring irregular verbs (Grammar Forge, Part A).
  • L.2.2 — Demonstrate command of capitalization, punctuation, and spelling (Grammar Forge, Part B).

Design notes: activities use large touch targets, single-click selection (no drag-and-drop) for accessibility, immediate non-punitive feedback with unlimited retries, color paired with icons/text (not color alone), and a progress trail so students always know how much remains.
